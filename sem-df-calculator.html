df = p(p+1)/2 − q for covariance structure models, or p(p+1)/2 + p − q when a mean structure is modeled, where p is the number of observed variables and q is the number of freely estimated parameters. With k groups, knowns scale to k × that quantity (each group contributes its own covariance matrix, and mean vector if means are modeled), while q only multiplies by k for parameters left free per group — parameters checked "equal across groups" count once. With L levels (multilevel/within-between models), each level can have its own indicator count and factor count, and contributes its own p₂(p₂+1)/2 covariance knowns; grand means are modeled only once, using the highest level's indicator count. df > 0 means the model is overidentified (the usual, testable case); df = 0 is just-identified (saturated, perfect fit by construction); df < 0 means the model is underidentified and cannot be fit as specified.
